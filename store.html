Shipping, Returns & FAQ

🚚 Shipping

We deliver across Tamil Nadu. After you place your order, we confirm it on WhatsApp and share the delivery charge and expected delivery time for your pincode.

↩️ Returns

If your pack arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp with a photo as soon as you receive it and we will help resolve it.

❓ FAQ

How do I place an order?

Open the Shop, add items to your cart, enter your delivery details and tap "Send WhatsApp Order". We will confirm it on WhatsApp.

Can I choose the pack size?

Yes. Most products come in more than one weight. Pick the size you like on the product card before adding it to the cart.

How do I contact you?

Tap "Contact Us" in the footer to chat with us directly on WhatsApp.
